# Tanegashima runtime asset

The P2.4 Coastal Release retains the current coastal campus, paths and launch animation. The export replaces the 90 inventoried inherited M01 parts and 369 unresolved Polish gantry parts with project-authored launch infrastructure and command buildings. The audit found zero matches for those inherited objects and materials. The 14 animation clips and site metadata match the local P2.4 baseline. Coastal Park paths, the research access arc, the command footpath and the service apron follow the exported terrain. Clearance is sampled at 3.5 cm or more. The east access road connects to the launch apron. The woodland keeps more of its authored tree placements and uses crossed cutout crowns on the far hillside.
